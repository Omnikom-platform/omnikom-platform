Client dashboard: four directions to pick from

Greyscale wireframes of the same three screens (login, leads inbox, page editor), each grounded in one real design reference and each with its own rail, grid, type and motion. Structure is fixed by the blueprint; only the look differs. Click a shot to open the full page; open the HTML itself to see the motion (toggle top right). Judged 2026-10-29; the Plain direction was dropped because it shared Document's rail, table and panel grid. Nothing here is coloured or branded yet: that comes after the pick.

1. Document (reference: Notion)

The dashboard reads like a calm page you write in: big title, quiet blocks, text-only page tree on the left, warm greys.

3. Inbox (reference: Intercom)

A lead is a thread: list on the left, the conversation on the right with status and owner at the top, the note box at the bottom. The editor reuses the same split.

Rail: workspace name on top, who is signed in at the bottomMotion: newest row drops inCheck: PASS 2 of 2, no defect seen
