## 1. What the deck is

A six-page pitch deck for Omnikom, a company that builds and runs websites for local businesses on its own platform. It is sent to a business owner before a sales call. It has to be read in two minutes. The deck exists to make one promise land: **everything we build for you is yours, even the day you leave.**

What a client gets:
- A website built for them, on their own domain, with a page for every place and service they want customers from. Fast on phones, readable by Google and by AI answer engines.
- Their own CRM inside the platform. Every enquiry from every form lands there the second it is sent, sorted by what the customer asked for, with status, owner, notes and a timeline. They can add phone calls by hand.
- An alert email to the person who should act, with the answers and a link to the lead.
- The ability to change their own words and photos, post articles, add listings, and publish. Every version is kept, anything can be undone.
- Their Google numbers in plain words, dated.
- Later, paid add-ons they can switch on: an AI concierge that calls a lead and books the meeting, meeting texts and emails, and export or import of leads in the format of whatever CRM they already use.

How it happens: seven steps (audit, draft, review, launch, listed, tune, mentions), then a monthly growth loop. Free search traffic first; paid ads only when free results level off.

Proof we can show, with sources:
- Google PageSpeed Insights on the latest site built on the platform, measured 29 October 2026: mobile 99, 99, 97, 99, 98 across five pages; desktop 100 on all five; accessibility 100.
- Our own test suite tries to break the wall between one client's data and another's in 126 different ways; every attempt is caught.

Pricing: no price list. The setup is quoted per client after the audit; the growth loop is monthly; add-ons are rented monthly, each priced on its own.

The exit terms, which are the heart of it: if a client leaves, they get the full code of their website at no charge. The site can stay live with Omnikom for a flat hosting fee, or move anywhere. Their login, their CRM and every lead stay theirs. Only the services they stop, stop.

Branding stance, in the owner's words: "we are not something hidden, we are enterprise." Every email the platform sends carries the client's brand on top and a small "powered by Omnikom" at the foot.

## 4. What I would like you to make

**Step A, a style board first.** Four distinct visual directions, each as one 16:9 image that shows how the cover could feel. Keep them far apart from each other, for example:
