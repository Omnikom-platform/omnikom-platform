# Compliance dossier: MLS listings (IDX) on a California broker website, 2026-10-27

Scope: what a California broker website must do to show MLS listings, show the broker's license, and collect leads.
Method: read-only web research on 2026-10-27 (WebSearch, WebFetch, local text extraction of PDFs). Nothing was submitted, no one was contacted, nothing was logged into.
Status words: CONFIRMED = official NAR / C.A.R. / MLS / government page read by me. REPORTED = third party, a mirror of an official source, or a search-engine summary I did not open at the source. UNCONFIRMED = not found or not verifiable.
This is research for a build team, not legal advice. Have the broker's compliance contact and the MLS data-licensing office approve the final display.

## Summary

- **The NAR handbook text I could read is the 35th edition (2023).** The current IDX page (Policy Statement 7.58) on nar.realtor is member-gated ("Login Required"), and the 2026 edition is sold in the NAR store. The 2023 text matches the 2026 rules of San Diego MLS (rules dated July 2026) and Bay East MLS (rules effective 2026-03-30), so the core has not moved. NAR made 18 handbook changes effective January 2026, but I could not see an itemised list, so I cannot rule out an IDX tweak.
- **Core IDX rules (NAR 7.58, mirrored in C.A.R. Model Rule 12.16 and the MLS rules):** refresh MLS downloads and displays at least every 12 hours; show the listing firm, the listing contact (email or phone) and in California the listing agent in a "reasonably prominent location" in a type not smaller than the median listing type; show the MLS as source plus the last-updated date; show the data-display disclaimer in 7 pt or larger; never show confidential fields; never modify listing data; never share the data with anyone the MLS has not authorised; the site must be under the participant's "actual and apparent control".
- **Attribution wording is a trap.** CRMLS (2026 standards, updated 2026-02-24) says do not use "Courtesy of"; San Diego MLS requires "Listed by" and bans "Presented by". Use "Listed by <firm>" and name the agent. The prompt's "Listing courtesy of" wording would fail CRMLS.
- **Sold data is already allowed.** I found no 2019 change. NAR's archived page says the 2015 policy required three years of sold data in IDX, and the handbook says MLSs must provide sold data from January 1, 2012 where it is publicly accessible, with the sale price withheld where sold prices are not public record. A broker may choose not to display sold.
- **Refresh rule conflicts across MLSs.** NAR and C.A.R. say 12 hours. Sibling dossiers report MetroList says 3 days and the bridgeMLS vendor agreement v6.0 says 72 hours. Build to 12 hours and check each MLS.
- **California license display:** B&P 10140.6 (text updated 2023) needs the licensee's name, license number and the responsible broker's identity on first-point-of-contact solicitation materials, which now expressly include electronic media. Regulation 2773, as printed by the DRE, names "websites owned, controlled, and/or maintained by the soliciting real estate licensee"; the license number must be no smaller than the smallest type on the page. MLS Rule 12.17 adds firm name and state of licensure on the website.
- **Team names:** a DRE team name needs a member's surname plus "group", "team" or "associates", cannot contain "broker" or "brokerage" words, and the responsible broker's name must be as prominent as the team name. DRE says a team name is not a fictitious business name.
- **VOW is a separate, login-gated product.** It needs a lawful broker-consumer relationship first, name plus verified email, terms of use, password expiry, and a privacy policy. In Bay East rules a VOW still may not show expired or withdrawn listings, so "expired listings" is not a reason to build a VOW there.
- **Privacy:** CCPA thresholds are now $26,625,000 revenue (CPPA adjustment effective 2025-01-01), 100,000 consumers or households, or 50% of revenue from selling or sharing data. A small brokerage is usually below, but CalOPPA (B&P 22575) applies to any commercial site collecting personal data from Californians: post a privacy policy. Federal TCPA applies regardless of size: SMS and auto-dialed marketing needs signed prior express written consent.
- **Fair housing and accessibility:** the Fair Housing Act ad rule (42 USC 3604(c)) is unchanged. HUD's April 29, 2024 digital-advertising guidance was withdrawn (Federal Register notice 2026-04-06, effective 2025-09-17), so treat it as background only. No official ADA website standard exists; WCAG 2.1 AA is the practical target, and California Unruh Act suits are the main exposure (REPORTED).
- **Gaps:** MLSListings, MetroList and BAREIS rule text on IDX display could not be read by me (sibling dossiers 02 and 03 cover BAREIS and MetroList). Which MLS the client belongs to is the first thing to confirm.
