# bridgeMLS (Bay East + CCAR + bridgeMLS) IDX feed dossier, 2026-10-27

Scope: how a broker gets an IDX data feed for a third-party website vendor, East Bay, California.
Method: read-only. Official MLS pages and PDFs were read on 2026-10-27 (PDFs text-extracted with pdftotext, so quotes are from the document itself). Nothing was submitted, no one was contacted, no log-in used.

## Summary

- **Two-party paperwork per site.** The broker (Participant) or agent (Subscriber) signs the IDX Data Access Agreement, AND the vendor signs the Consultant's Data Access Agreement. bridgeMLS says each website needs both. Bay East has its own 2-page application (page 1 agent + broker, page 2 vendor); CCAR routes everything "through your selected vendor". CONFIRMED.
- **Approved-vendor lists exist at all three bodies, and Brivity is on none of them.** bridgeMLS (about 130 names, PDF generated 2026-10-16), Bay East (29 names) and CCAR lists were all read. A vendor not on a list can still apply (Bay East says so). So Brivity's presence on the client's current site is not explained by public documents (see Could not confirm).
- **bridgeMLS is NOT Bridge Interactive (Zillow).** Same word, different organisations; Zillow's own 2018 release says "two different entities". bridgeMLS runs on Paragon (Black Knight / Clareity log-ins). Feed mechanics are mixed evidence: bridgeMLS's vendor agreement still says RETS or IDX-EZ framing; its IDX policy names a "RESO Web API"; a third party (mlsimport.com) reports credentials come from Paragon's "Property Information Central" (paragonapi.com). Treat the delivery API as UNCONFIRMED until bridgeMLS support names the endpoint. Design the ingest layer behind an adapter (RESO Web API / OData first, RETS fallback).
- **Refresh rule is 12 hours in current MLS rules** (Bay East rules dated 2026-06-16, bridgeMLS IDX page, NorCAL combined IDX guidelines, CCAR page). The bridgeMLS vendor agreement v6.0 (rev. 11/17/2025) still says 72 hours and the old policy v4.1 says 3 days. Build to 12 h. Vendor pull limit is 6 updates a day; full downloads only after 6 pm Mon-Fri.
- **Money (vendor side):** $145 set-up per new bridgeMLS user onboarded and a "$3,000 per year BBO/DAA fee" (clause 45, payer not stated) are in the vendor agreement. Broker side: IDX is no-cost (Bay East: member benefit at no cost). Third-party quotes: iHomefinder $125 set-up (CCAR members exempt); IDX Broker $145 + $6/month (old page).
- **Display must-haves:** the combined disclaimer, listing agent + listing office + contact, "Data last updated" stamp, no "MLS" in the URL, brokerage name + DRE number, sold limited to 3 years, no Days on Market (combined IDX), no sold remarks / list price / extra photos (policy v4.1). All CONFIRMED below.
- **One feed for Solano/Napa/Sacramento is possible but is a separate licence track:** NorCAL MLS Alliance "Combined IDX" (BAREIS, MetroList, SFAR, MLSListings, Bay East, CCAR, bridgeMLS). It needs a Data Request Form, an Authorization Form, and an individual licence with each contributing MLS. MetroList additionally requires the broker to be a MetroList Participant and allows co-mingling transport only. The bridgeMLS-only agreement does NOT cover those counties.

## Process as we understand it

1. The client broker (Participant, broker of record) must be an active bridgeMLS (or Bay East / CCAR) MLS member. Agents (Subscribers) need the broker's signature.
2. Pick a vendor from the approved lists, or have a new vendor apply. For our own build the vendor is Omnikom: an unlisted vendor must apply and be registered as an IDX Consultant. Allow weeks, not days, until bridgeMLS says otherwise.
3. The broker signs the IDX Data Access Agreement for Participant/Subscriber (bridgeMLS) and/or the Bay East IDX application (Form 08/2025). The vendor signs the Consultant's Data Access Agreement v6.0 (one per client website). Both go to bridgeMLS (mail/fax per the form; confirm the email route with support@bridgemls.com) and to idx@bayeast.org for Bay East data. CCAR routes paperwork through the vendor.
4. The MLS countersigns. bridgeMLS states 3 business days after receipt; Bay East 2 days to 4 weeks depending on vendor status.
5. The MLS issues access (RETS credentials, IDX-EZ link, or RESO Web API credentials; platform not confirmed). The vendor pays set-up ($145 per new user) and any annual fee ($3,000 BBO/DAA clause, Bay East annual licence).
6. The vendor ingests within limits (max 6 update pulls a day, full pulls only after 6 pm weekdays) and refreshes the public site at least every 12 h.
7. The site goes live with: brokerage name and DRE number, combined disclaimer + "Data last updated", listing agent + office + contact, no "MLS" in the URL, hidden fields respected, sold limits applied.
8. The vendor emails the monthly client list to bridgeMLS and Bay East by the 5th. The MLS monitors; violations get 5 business days to fix, then removal.
9. Only if the client also wants Solano/Napa/Sacramento: add a separate NorCAL Combined IDX request (Data Request Form, Authorization Form, a licence with each contributing MLS; MetroList also requires broker MetroList membership).
