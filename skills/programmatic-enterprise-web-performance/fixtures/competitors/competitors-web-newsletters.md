# Competitors: websites and newsletters

Saved 2026-10-25 from a helper agent's final answer (sources inside).

**Method.** I used WebSearch and WebFetch only, with a few pages per site. I did not read robots.txt files. No sign-ins or forms were used.

**Limits.**
- mansionglobal.com, architecturaldigest.com and jamesedition.com blocked fetches or the search crawler. Therealdeal.com and architectureforsale.com also returned 403s. Robb Report's fetch redirect failed on DNS. Those rows rest on search-result titles and URLs or third-party reports, and I mark them.
- I had no rank data. Ahrefs and Similarweb are unauthenticated here. "What they appear for" below is the page types and titles the search tool returned, not measured positions.
- Almost nobody publishes a feature price. Only one real price exists, at LuxVT (row 21). Other figures are labelled where they appear.
- I found no US equivalent of The Modern House. A search for a US launch by The Modern House found nothing. The closest US matches are partial: brokerage-run editorial (Beyond Shelter, Crosby Doe), media titles with weekly listing roundups (Dwell, The Spaces), and regional publishers selling "sponsored listing" features (Chronogram Media).

## The 8 closest, and the page types they use

I cannot rank sites by measured search position. These are the page types returned for relevant searches.

1. **dwell.com.**
   - Weekly roundup "On the Market: [highlights] and More Great Homes for Sale This Week". It has five homes, each with the address, price and listing agent, plus a "Learn More" link to a full article.
   - Single-home pieces like `/article/neutra-for-sale-65544342`.
   - Style hubs `/homes/midcentury` and `/homes/modern`, and the tag `/homes/query/homes-for-sale`.
2. **offthemrkt.com.** "Our Favorite Listings of the Week", dated by week, five homes with price, agent, brokerage and link, ending in a newsletter signup.
3. **ruralintelligence.com.**
   - Single-home pieces `/sponsored-<slug>/` and `/sponsored-listing-<slug>/`, labelled in the headline.
   - A hub `/tag/featured-real-estate` mixing paid and editorial entries.
4. **beyondshelter.com.** Landing pages per style, architect and neighbourhood: `/mid-century-homes-for-sale-los-angeles/`, `/john-lautner/`, `/tag/bel-air-homes-for-sale/`.
5. **thespaces.com.**
   - Root-level headline slugs, e.g. `/a-1783-stone-house-on-155-acres-is-for-sale-in-new-yorks-catskills/`.
   - Headlines start "For sale:" or "Buy ...".
   - Roundups like "Go west: shop these eight modern California listings".
6. **upstater.com** and **upstatehouse.com.** "On the Market" by price band, "Five-Figure Fridays", and features marked "Presented by" a brokerage.
7. **architectureforsale.com** and **crosbydoe.com.** Architect-named houses, press pages like "LA Magazine: Modern Masters", and news coverage of Neutra and Lautner sales. Evidence is thin because the fetch failed.
8. **robbreport.com.**
   - `/shelter/homes-for-sale/<architect-and-place>-<id>/`.
   - Headlines with the architect, the verb "Lists" or "Just Listed", and the price: "A Rocky Mountain Masterpiece by Architect Hugh Newell Jacobsen Lists for $12.8 Million".
   - Separate `/listing/<address>/` pages.

Runners-up: wallpaper.com, the modernist-collective Substack, and brownstoner.com.

**Page types that recur across the set:**
- An architect or style plus "for sale" in the title.
- Price in the headline.
- A weekly roundup of 4 to 5 homes ending in a newsletter signup.
- A landing page per architect or style.
- Paid items labelled "Sponsored" or "Presented by".

**Not verified:**
- The Modern House's pricing, and any Matter of Place-style per-feature fee. None is public except at LuxVT.
- AD and Mansion Global page types.
- Whether newsletters exist at Brownstoner, Eichler Network, and The Real Deal.

Lived In, Domicile and The Freestone Newsletter turned up as design and real-estate newsletters, but none showed individual homes for sale, so I left them out.
