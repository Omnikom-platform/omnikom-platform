# Competitors: paid media, agencies, brokerage programs

Saved 2026-10-25 from a helper agent's final answer (sources inside).

I found 25 sellers across the three groups. Only a handful of single-feature prices are public, and I could read about half of the pages myself.

Many sites returned 403 or a bot wall: hauteliving.com, forbesglobalproperties.com, luxuryportfolio.com, compass.com, sothebysrealty.com, mansionglobal.com, robbreport.com (redirected to a bot-wall host) and the Christie's marketing page. I did not try a browser tier on them, as you asked for WebSearch and WebFetch only.

- **BLOCKED:** Robb Report and Mansion Global current rate cards. Unblocking them needs the media kits from their ad sales, or a logged-in browser fetch.

- **Marked "F":** facts I fetched and read on the page.

- **Webtonic roundup:** a competitor agency's SEO article. It got one agency wrong (Luxury Listing Video is a Florida shop with no listed price, not an LA studio with a price). I use it only for leads and mark its prices as unverified.

## Gaps Matter of Place could own

1. **A public price for one home's feature.** Almost every seller says "contact us". The numbers that exist are memberships (Haute, $500 to $6,000 a year), pay-at-close ($195 plus $1,000), and per-copy magazines. A flat, posted per-feature price would stand alone.
2. **Selective, written editorial across CA, NY and FL.** Today the choice is a regional magazine for one metro (Digs, Behind The Hedges, Palm Beach Illustrated), a national portal with ads (Unique Homes, duPont Registry, Mansion Global), or a social post plus a Google News item (Haute). I found no one selling an independent, declinable story with photography, newsletter and social as one purchase.
3. **Not a brokerage.** Sotheby's, Christie's, Coldwell Banker, Compass, Elliman and The Agency tie exposure to a listing agreement with their brand. An owner or agent at any firm could buy Matter of Place without switching, which fits the "we got you in" test.
4. **Plain disclosure.** Haute says it does not guarantee rankings, leads or sales. Few others say whether a feature is paid, and none I found separates the fee from the sale outcome. "Paid, labelled, no outcome promised" can be the stated standard.
5. **Writing and print together.** Agencies sell film (C&I), PR (RUHM), a book or deck (Sher Joniff), or print (Unique Homes) separately. A single story with a printed edition, not a render-only deck, is open. Discretion is also open: Compass owns "private", and nobody sells a before-the-market or after-the-sale story.
