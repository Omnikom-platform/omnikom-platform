**Patterns**
1. **Money:** There are three models. Paid listing packages: CIRCA at $55 plus add-ons, Historical Homes at $50–$250, and Haute and Brownstoner sponsored features. Reader subscriptions: Cheap Old Houses at $12/month. Brand ads and TV: Zillow Gone Wild, Cheap Old Houses and the HGTV shows. The design-led brokerages (The Modern House, This Old Hudson, The Value of Architecture) are paid by commission, not a feature fee.
2. **Formats:** Carousels dominate the feeds I fetched. Reels and clips come second. Highlights are used for "Submit", "Newsletter", "Sold" and named properties. Each account links to a newsletter, a submission form or a Linktree.
3. **Words:** Bios use "design-led", "historic", "curated", "most beautiful", "as featured in New York mag", "Connecting Old House Lovers To Their Dreams" and "Obsessed? Join 233,083 subscribers". Hashtags are UNPROVEN because captions were not visible on the fetched pages.
4. **Gaps:** I found no Florida-specific design-led listing curator and no Pinterest or TikTok account that fits. Both are open space. The historic-home aggregators (rows 8 and 9) are volume plays, not curated.
5. **Closest eight:** @circahouses, @cheapoldhouses, @thisoldhudson, @themodernhouse, @thevalueofarchitecture, @brownstoner, I Know a Spot, and @historicalhomesofamerica. It is the paid-exposure twin of CIRCA, though less curated. @fortheloveofoldhouses is next if you want a ninth.
