How to steep oolong tea

Oolong rewards patience: the leaves are rolled tight and need a little time and hot water to unfurl.

Leaf and water

Use about one rounded teaspoon of leaf for each cup, and water just under the boil.

Timing the infusions

The first infusion runs for about a minute, and each later one a little longer than the one before.
