# DSN-007 (P1, design tells): greeting headlines

Source: skills/unslop/references/signs.md:43 and skills/unslop/SKILL.md:106; planning/DECISIONS.md 37 (owner, 2026-10-31); unslop by Damir Sokolovsky, CC BY 4.0
Check: `checks/dsn-007.mjs`. Fixtures (folders under `fixtures/`): bad-DSN-007, and bad-DSN (every tell on one page).
This rule is DECISIONS 37's list of design tells made into a check; the list comes from the open skill unslop by Damir Sokolovsky
(github.com/heydamir/unslop, folder skills/unslop, CC BY 4.0). The rule text below is written for this engine in our own words.
Severity P1; the verdict of each finding is FAIL. A finding names the rule, the page, the line, the exact text or class that tripped it, and the source.

## Rule text
The text of an `h1`, `h2` or `h3` does not start with `Hello`, `Hi`, `Hey`, `Welcome`, `Greetings` or `Howdy` as a whole word, in any case.
A word counts as a whole word when the next character is not a letter, digit, underscore, hyphen or apostrophe: "Hi, I am Sam" and "Welcome to
Brindlewood" fail; "Hi-fi speakers", "Highlights" and "Heythrop Hall" pass.

## Judged here
The first word of the heading text only.

## Not judged here
Greetings in `h4` to `h6` or in other elements, in other languages, or that are not the first word. Text inside `svg`, `template`, `noscript`, `script`, `style` and comments is never judged. A snapshot (a live site saved by the skill's snapshot script) carries each page's HTML only, so in a snapshot this rule judges what the
HTML carries (classes, style attributes, `<style>` blocks, text); the site's stylesheets are not in the snapshot and are not judged.
