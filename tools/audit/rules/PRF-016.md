## Rule text
Per route group of non-programmatic routes, warm TTFB <= `ttfbMs`; the spec measures the median of 5 warm requests after one discarded request.

## Judged here (snapshot mode only)
- Per judged page (status 200), `response.json.ttfbMs` against `speed.ttfbMs` (default 600, config like PRF-001). Over the budget is a WARN
  `budget-exceeded` that says "single cold request": the snapshot makes one cold request per page, not the spec's median of 5 warm ones, so this
  check can never FAIL a run. A page with no `ttfbMs` is `SKIP ... not measured`.

## Not judged here

prints no line there. The other numbers of the defaults block (`pageBytes 1638400`, `htmlBytes 102400`, PRF-019) are out of scope.
