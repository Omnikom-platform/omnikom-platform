# MET-003 (P0, web-delivery)

## Rule text
Exactly one `<main>` with exactly one `<h1>` on public pages (part main; missing-element, duplicate-element);
part skip: in document order across the whole `<body>`, each `<hN>` after the first heading has N <= the previous heading's level + 1
(going up any number of levels is allowed; value-mismatch with both headings).

## Judged here
- part main: `<main>` count is 1 and `<h1>` count (whole page) is 1. FAIL at the second element's line, or at the `<body>` line when none.
- part skip: every heading after the first, in document order across the whole page (header, nav, main, footer), has a level at most
  one more than the heading before it. The FAIL names both headings.

## Not judged here
Nothing of this rule is left out. (A heading inside `<svg>`, `<template>`, `<noscript>` or a comment is not a heading of the page.)
